# Zeta brand — for marketing content

The brand files themselves live in [`/branding`](../../branding/README.md) and
are generated by `scripts/brand/`. This page is the short version for anyone
(or any AI) making a post, poster or video.

## Name, line and voice

- **Name:** Zeta. Website: **zetastruct.app** (`https://www.zetastruct.app`).
- **Campaign line:** *Structural engineering, connected.* Supporting: *Model. Analyze. Design. Detail. Deliver.*
- **Logo lockup tagline (unchanged):** *Structural engineering made clearer.* Use the lockup as it is; the campaign line goes in the post, not into the logo.
- **Voice:** an engineer explaining to an engineer. Concrete nouns, real numbers with their units, the clause when it matters. No hype words ("revolutionary", "instant", "AI-powered"), no claims outside `../verification/approved-marketing-claims.md`.
- **Spelling:** US English in campaign copy (*analyze*, *color*), SI units with a thin space or a space before the unit (`261 kN·m`, `175 mm`).

(10/64 of the mark) on every side. Minimum 20 px mark height in a lockup.

## Type

- **Display & body:** Archivo (800 for headlines, tracked −3.5 %; 400–600 for text).
- **Labels, numbers, URL:** IBM Plex Mono (500–600, uppercase labels tracked +12–16 %).
- Both are OFL fonts; local copies in `scripts/showreel/fonts/`.
- On a 1080×1920 canvas: headline 76–86 px, body ≥ 27 px, labels ≥ 19 px. Nothing essential below 19 px.

## Layout — 9:16 vertical

- Canvas **1080 × 1920**. Everything essential inside x 90–990, y 150–1640.
- The bottom 280 px carry only the URL and a small tag — Reels/Stories UI covers it.
- Top: logo left, the **ACTUAL ZETA OUTPUT** stamp right whenever the image is a capture of the app. A conceptual illustration carries **ILLUSTRATION** instead.
- One idea per poster: kicker → headline → the real artifact → one line of explanation → the verified inputs in mono.
- Ground: ink gradient with the faint drafting grid (180 px major, 36 px minor).

## What every image must be

1. A capture of the running app (`../evidence/`), or
2. a conceptual engineering illustration labelled as one.

Never an invented interface, an edited result, or a number not in
`../verification/verified-examples.md`.
